# power_strip.glb

Original RetroXR asset: made from scratch by `Tools/glb/make_power_strip.py` (Blender 5.2),
so it has the project's own licence and needs no third-party credit. Re-run that script
to regenerate it rather than editing the GLB.

A 6-outlet US strip (NEMA 5-15R, grounded), 290 × 52 × 38 mm, with a red rocker switch.
The cord, its strain relief and the plug are built in RetroXR: `CordAnchor` is on the +X (switch) end face, and its local -Z points out along the cord.
