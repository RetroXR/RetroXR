Super Famicom / PAL Super Nintendo cartridge shell (sfc_cart.glb)

Own work. Ryan McClelland's photographs and caliper measurements of a Super
Famicom cartridge (Super Mario RPG, SHVC-006), rebuilt as a parametric Blender
model registered to those photographs and reduced for real-time use (the
debranded mobile tier: 20,768 triangles, 128 x 87.5 x 20 mm). The PAL cartridge
is the same shell. The source .blend files, build scripts and references live
outside this repository (codex-photos/snes-cart-jap/cartridge_assets:
build_all.py, export_retroxr.py). Plastic grain and the rear ribbing are tangent
normal maps; the Label mesh carries a plain white placeholder that the cartridge
script replaces with the scraped label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

No third-party marks are carried. The moulded Nintendo oval and its (R) on the
back are left off, and the rear warning sticker (recreated, not scanned) keeps
its warning text, CASSETTE, MODEL NO. SHVC-006 and its serial without the
"SUPER FAMICOM(R)" mark or the trademark notice line. The moulded
PAT. PEND. / MADE IN JAPAN stays.
