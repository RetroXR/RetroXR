Sega 32X cartridge shell (sega32x_cart.glb)

Own work. Ryan McClelland's photographs of a US Sega 32X cartridge (Virtua
Fighter), rebuilt as a parametric Blender model registered to those photographs
and reduced for real-time use (the clean mobile tier: 6,168 triangles,
112.8 x 73 x 17 mm). The shell's size, curved front and recess outlines were
measured on a 1:1 reference model, RetroGameRevival's "SEGA 32X Cartridge Shell"
(printables.com/model/845316, CC BY-NC); it was used for measurement only and
none of its geometry is in this model. The source .blend files, build scripts
and references live outside this repository (codex-photos/sega-32x-cart/asset:
build_cartridge.py, export_retroxr.py). The plastic's fine grain is a tangent
normal map; the Label mesh carries a plain white placeholder that the cartridge
script replaces with the scraped label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

No third-party marks are carried. The moulded SEGA logo and its TM, and the
moulded CAUTION text on the back (which names the Genesis 32X), are left off;
their recessed plaques stay blank.
