Game Boy Color cartridge (gbc_cart_large.glb, gbc_cart_small.glb)

Own work. The clear Game Boy Color-only cartridge shell (model CGB-002),
built from Ryan McClelland's caliper measurements and photographs of a
cartridge in codex-photos/nintendo/gbc/cart/photos (build_gbc.py, export_retroxr.py): the
debranded mobile level of detail, one per circuit board. The shell exports
solid; RetroXR makes it clear smoke, or Pokemon Crystal's clear blue with
glitter, at runtime. No label artwork is included: the Label mesh carries a
plain white placeholder that the cartridge script covers with the scraped
label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

The front "GAME BOY COLOR" logo, the rear "Nintendo" oval and the
"Nintendo" silkscreen on the board are left off; the model number, patent
and country markings remain.

Circuit board photographs (embedded in the GLBs, and the textures Godot
extracts from them), from the Game Boy hardware database
(https://gbhwdb.gekkio.fi/cartridges/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ :
- gbc_cart_large.glb: DMG-KGDU-10, by ikk5, from the entry for Pokemon
  Crystal (CGB-BYTP-1),
  https://gbhwdb.gekkio.fi/cartridges/CGB-BYTP-1/ikk5-1.html
- gbc_cart_small.glb: DMG-A09-10, by Gekkio, from the entry for Shrek: Fairy
  Tale Freakdown (CGB-BFUP-0),
  https://gbhwdb.gekkio.fi/cartridges/CGB-BFUP-0/gekkio-1.html
Changes: perspective-corrected and registered to the board's millimetres,
resampled to 20 px/mm, the lighting gradient evened out, the backdrop beyond
the board and in its holes filled with board colour, and the "Nintendo"
silkscreen wordmark on the trace side removed. These derived textures are
shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
