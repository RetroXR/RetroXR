Nintendo 64 cartridge, USA and Japan bodies
(n64_cartridge_usa.glb, n64_cartridge_jpn.glb)

Own work. Modelled in Blender from Ryan McClelland's photographs of NTSC-U and
NTSC-J cartridges and reduced for real-time use; the source .blend files, build
scripts and references live outside this repository. Plastic grain is a tangent
normal map; the front Label mesh carries a plain white placeholder that the
cartridge script replaces with the scraped label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

No third-party marks are carried. The moulded wordmark on the rear shell is left
off, its oval blank, and the rear stickers (n64_cartridge_usa_rear_usa.png,
n64_cartridge_jpn_rear_jpn.png) keep their warning text without the Nintendo 64
emblem, the wordmark or a Nintendo copyright line. They are modified artwork, not
reproductions of an original print.
