Game Boy cartridge (gb_cart.glb)

Own work. Modelled in Blender from Ryan McClelland's photographs and
measurements of a DMG cartridge and reduced for real-time use (the Quest close
export, 14,386 triangles); the source .blend files and references live outside
this repository. Plastic grain is a tangent normal map; the Label mesh carries a
plain white placeholder that the cartridge script replaces with the scraped
label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

No third-party marks are carried. The moulded Nintendo and GAME BOY lettering
above the label is left off and its bowl left blank; the rear MADE IN JAPAN and
PAT. PEND. markings remain.
