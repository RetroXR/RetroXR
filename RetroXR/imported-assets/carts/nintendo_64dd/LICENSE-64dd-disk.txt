64DD disk (n64dd_disk.glb)

Own work. Modeled from the Internet Archive's scans of a 64DD disk (the
Communication Kit disk's front and back) and photographs, built parametrically
in the RetroXR-models repository (nintendo/n64/64dd-disk: its debranded first
mobile level, n64dd_disk_mobile_clean.glb) and turned into RetroXR's floppy
frame (label +Z, shutter +Y) by Tools/glb/prepare_64dd_disk.py. Both labels,
Label in the front recess and LabelTop on the trailing edge, are plain white
paper that the cartridge script lays the scraped art on.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

No third-party marks are carried. The Nintendo oval on the shutter face is left
blank, its field kept, and the caution label is traced artwork with its warning
text and model number only: the 64DD logo and the trademark, copyright and
Randnet lines are left off. It is modified artwork, not a reproduction of an
original print.

It replaces Mickemoose's CC BY 4.0 disk, which RetroXR shipped before.
