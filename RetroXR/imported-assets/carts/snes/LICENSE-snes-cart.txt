Super NES cartridge, North American shell (snes_cart_type_a.glb, snes_cart_type_b.glb)

Own work. Ryan McClelland's own Blender model of the cartridge, refined against
his photographs and caliper measurements of a Super Mario World (Type A, the
narrow latch groove) and a Bubsy II (Type B, the broad recess) cartridge, and
reduced for real-time use (the mobile tier: 17,285 and 18,377 triangles). The
source .blend files, build scripts and references live outside this repository
(codex-photos/snes-cart: rebuild_from_user.py, package_revision2.py,
export_retroxr.py). Plastic grain is a tangent normal map; the Label mesh
carries a plain white placeholder that the cartridge script replaces with the
scraped label art.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

No third-party marks are carried. The moulded Nintendo oval on the back is left
off, and the rear warning sticker (recreated, not scanned) keeps its warning
text, MODEL NO. SNS-006, SNS-USA and MADE IN JAPAN without the
"Rd. (M) (C)1991 NINTENDO" line.
