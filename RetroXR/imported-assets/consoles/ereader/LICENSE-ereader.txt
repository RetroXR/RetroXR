e-Reader (ereader.glb, ereader_lod1.glb, ereader_lod2.glb)

Own work. Modelled from Ryan McClelland's photographs of a US e-Reader
(AGB-014) and his caliper measurements of it: 94 x 112 x 42.6 mm with the
cartridge tongue. Built in Blender by codex-photos/ereader/build_ereader.py,
then reduced for mobile by build_ereader_lods.py (the "mobile_debranded"
package): hidden faces culled, decimated, and the detailed surface baked into
one atlas (base colour, tangent normal, packed ORM) plus a sticker texture.

  ereader.glb       LOD0  3,938 triangles  1024 px maps
  ereader_lod1.glb  LOD1  2,349 triangles   512 px maps
  ereader_lod2.glb  LOD2  1,468 triangles   256 px maps

All three share the root name, origin and axes (+Y up, the card-slot face on
+Z). LOD1 and LOD2 cap the screw wells, latch pockets and EXT ports flush and
bake them in as shading.

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

Debranded: the e-Reader logo is left off both stickers, the molded Nintendo
logo is left off the rear shell (it is not in the geometry or any texture),
and the rear sticker's two copyright lines (Nintendo / Creatures / HAL
Laboratory, and Olympus Optical) are removed. The model number, the FCC and
ICES notice, "PES-USA", "MADE IN JAPAN" and the molded EXT lettering remain.
