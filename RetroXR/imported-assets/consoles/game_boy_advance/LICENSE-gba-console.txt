Game Boy Advance console (gba_console.glb)

Own work. Reconstructed in Blender from Ryan McClelland's photographs and
caliper measurements of a Game Boy Advance, with the shell fitted to a laser
scan of a real one (codex-photos/nintendo/gba/console, build_console.py). This is the mobile
LOD0 of that asset, without trademarks (build_console_mobile.py, package
console_mobile_no_trademarks): 35,227 triangles, every part decimated and its
detail baked into texture atlases (base colour, tangent normal, packed ORM;
2048 shell and stickers, 1024 controls and details).

Licensed with the rest of RetroXR (see the repository LICENSE).

Left off: the molded Nintendo logo and its oval on the front, the printed
GAME BOY ADVANCE logo on the lens, the GAME BOY ADVANCE logo and
"(C) (M) 2000 Nintendo" on the rear rating label, and the support line on the
battery warning sticker. The model number, the compliance and country text and
the molded START, SELECT, POWER, EXT, PHONES and VOLUME legends remain.

The lettering is baked into the textures from glyphs rendered in Blender:
Bahnschrift (the rear label's rating and compliance lines and the button
letters) and Arial (the other legends), both Microsoft system fonts. No font
file is included.

Nodes keep the detailed asset's names and pivots: DPad, Button_A, Button_B,
Start, Select, Shoulder_L/R (hinges), PowerSwitch (slide), VolumeWheel (axle),
PowerLED, Screen (a 61.2 x 40.8 mm quad, full 0-1 UVs), ScreenGlass and
CartridgeSocket (where gba_cart.glb seats at identity).
