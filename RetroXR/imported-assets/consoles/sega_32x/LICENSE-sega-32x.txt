Sega 32X (sega32x.glb) and its Genesis Model 2 spacer (sega32x_spacer.glb)

Own work. Built from Ryan McClelland's laser scan of a Sega 32X: the two
housing halves, the cartridge plug's two halves, the slot's cartridge guide and
its two dust flaps, each registered to the others and reduced for real-time use
(the distance level of the build, 24,688 triangles, with 1024 x 1024 normal and
occlusion maps baked from the ~9M-triangle scan). The scan carries no colour, so
the plastic is a material. The source scan, the build scripts and the references
live outside this repository (codex-photos/sega/32x/console); the files shipped here are
its sega32x_unbranded_lod1.glb and sega32x_model2_spacer.glb (1,920 triangles),
by Tools/glb/prepare_sega32x.py (which also tidies the 32X's node names and adds
its PlugSeat and CartFloor markers).

Not from the scan, modelled to photographs of the real unit and Sega's service
manual: the metal shield in the plug's windows, the circuit board and its edge
contacts, the rear A/V OUT / A/V IN (9-pin mini-DIN) and ADAPTOR sockets, the
screws, the clean A/V OUT / A/V IN / ADAPTOR lettering, a dark interior liner
that stops the hollow shell from being seen through its gaps, and the Genesis
Model 2 spacer (sega32x_spacer.glb, codex-photos sega-32x/build_spacer.py) -- built
from a photo of Sega's riser and the scan's underside collar, its height set by
the gap under the unit on RetroXR's Model 2 Genesis.
