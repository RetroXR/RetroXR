MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as genesis_console.glb, by Tools/glb/prepare_genesis_textures.py
and Tools/glb/prepare_genesis.py:

  * Removed the trademarks the source paints into its textures: the GENESIS
    badge beside the cartridge slot, the SEGA plate on the front control strip,
    and the SEGA logo on the underside label. Each was painted over in the
    base colour, normal and metallic-roughness maps alike, so its embossed
    relief went with it.

  * Removed the three studio lights the Sketchfab scene ships.

  * Split the front control strip into the strip and its two caps
    (ButtonPower, ButtonReset) so each can be pressed on its own, and renamed
    every part to what it is (ShellTop, ShellBottom, CartSlot, SlotFlapFront,
    SlotFlapRear, PortsInside, Labels, ControlPanel). Each slot flap's origin
    was moved onto its hinge so it can swing open.

  * Baked the scene's node transforms into the geometry and rescaled it to
    real-world size (220 mm wide), centred on its own footprint and resting
    on y = 0, facing +Z.

  * Decimated for standalone VR from 156,484 to 30,056 triangles (Blender
    Collapse via Tools/glb/decimate_glb.py), with the shading re-derived from
    the new geometry, and every texture resized to 1024 x 1024.

NOT modified: the underside label's plain text ("GENESIS SYSTEM CONSOLE",
"SEGA OF AMERICA INC", the model, rating and serial lines) remains.
