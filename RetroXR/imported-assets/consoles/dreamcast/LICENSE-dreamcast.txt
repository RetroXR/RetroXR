MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Produced by Tools/glb/scrub_dreamcast_textures.py and Tools/glb/prepare_dreamcast.py,
then Tools/glb/fix_unmapped_uvs.py (33 unmapped triangles given UVs).

TRADEMARK REMOVAL

  * Deleted the raised Dreamcast swirl-and-wordmark sticker on the disc lid (three
    loose parts). The lid surface beneath it is the author's, unchanged.
  * Erased "SEGA" and "Compatible with Microsoft Windows CE" from the front of the
    case, from both the Case base-colour sheet and the Case normal map, where they
    were embossed. A second "SEGA" on an interior wall behind the front panel was
    erased from the same two sheets.

  The functional legends are KEPT: POWER, OPEN, the controller port letters A-D, and
  the rear LINE, AC IN, AV OUT and SERIAL.

GEOMETRY AND SCENE CHANGES

  * Deleted the disc. RetroXR builds discs from MediaDimensions at runtime.
  * Deleted the three area lights and the camera.
  * Flattened the node hierarchy, baked its non-uniform node scales into the meshes
    and rescaled by 0.071 m per source unit. The console lands at 190.1 x 80.2 x
    195.2 mm (W x H x D). It is centred on its footprint and rests on y = 0.
  * Made the case material opaque. It carried full transmission with alpha blending
    and a 0.18 base-colour factor that together read as smoked glass. The factor is
    now 1.0.
  * Gave the front power lens a material of its own (PowerLED).
  * Moved the controller port sockets +0.17 mm across and +1.29 mm up, so their wells
    sit centred in the case's front opening; they had run into its lower lip. The
    port plate moves up with them, stays centred, and its blank lower strip is drawn
    up to z = 18.2 mm so the plate sits on the flat front face instead of overhanging
    the curved base.
  * Separated the single Buttons mesh into PowerButton and OpenButton, re-origined at
    each cap's base. Joined the lid with its hinge arms into Lid, origined on the
    hinge axis.
  * Re-origined Spindle and SpindlePlate on their own axis.
  * Added empty marker nodes ControllerPort1-4 and AvOut at the connector mouths.
  * Downsized the textures from 4096 to 2048 px.
  * Renamed nodes to Case, Bottom, Lid, PowerButton, OpenButton, PowerLED,
    ControllerPorts, BackPorts, DiscReader, DiscReaderTop, DiscLaser, Spindle and
    SpindlePlate.
  The console shell is otherwise unchanged geometry by the original author.
